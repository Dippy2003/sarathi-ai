# NIC Renewal

## Eligibility / Branches
For citizens whose National Identity Card (NIC) is still in their possession but is
expiring, damaged in a way that is still legible, or needs a details update (name,
address, photo). This is distinct from NIC Replacement, which covers a **lost** or
**badly damaged/illegible** card.

## Required Documents
- Original NIC (the one being renewed)
- Birth certificate (original + copy)
- Two recent passport-size photographs
- Grama Niladhari certification of the application (Form to be certified by your GN)
- Marriage certificate, if surname changed due to marriage

## Steps
1. Obtain the NIC application form (Form C or online via the Department for
   Registration of Persons e-services portal).
2. Get the form certified by your area's Grama Niladhari, confirming your identity
   and address.
3. Submit the certified form with required documents at the nearest District
   Secretariat or the Department for Registration of Persons head office in
   Battaramulla.
4. Pay the applicable fee at submission.
5. Collect a receipt with a reference number for tracking.
6. Collect the new NIC either in person or by registered post, depending on the
   option chosen at submission.

## Fees
- Normal service: LKR 100 (approx.; verify current rate at submission)
- Urgent/same-day service (Battaramulla head office only): higher fee, verify
  current rate

## Submission Location
- Nearest District Secretariat, or
- Department for Registration of Persons (DRP) Head Office, Battaramulla, for
  urgent service
