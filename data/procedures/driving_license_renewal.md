# Driving License Renewal

## Eligibility / Branches
For license holders whose driving license is expiring or has expired, and who
still hold the physical license. This differs from a lost-license replacement,
which requires a police report and follows a separate process at the Department
of Motor Traffic (DMT).

- **Normal renewal**: license in hand, applying before or shortly after expiry.
- **Renewal after long expiry**: if the license has been expired for an extended
  period, a medical fitness certificate may additionally be required.

## Required Documents
- Current driving license (original)
- NIC (original + copy)
- Medical fitness certificate (required for renewals after long expiry, or for
  applicants above a certain age threshold — verify current age/expiry rules)
- One recent passport-size photograph

## Steps
1. Obtain the driving license renewal application form from a DMT office or the
   DMT website.
2. If required (long-expired license or age threshold), obtain a medical fitness
   certificate from an approved medical officer.
3. Submit the form with required documents at a Department of Motor Traffic office
   or an authorized DMT service center.
4. Pay the applicable renewal fee.
5. Provide biometrics/photograph capture if requested.
6. Collect the renewed license, either same-day or by post depending on the office.

## Fees
- Standard renewal fee, varies by license validity period chosen (5-year or other);
  verify current rate at the DMT office

## Submission Location
- Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) Head Office, Werahera, or an authorized DMT
  service center / district office
