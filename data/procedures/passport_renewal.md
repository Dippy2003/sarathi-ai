# Passport Renewal

## Eligibility / Branches
For citizens whose passport is expiring, expired, or full of visa pages, and who
hold their existing (or last-issued) passport. This differs from a lost-passport
report, which requires an additional police report step before renewal can proceed.

- **Normal renewal**: existing passport is in hand.
- **Lost passport branch**: police report required before applying, additional
  declaration needed.

## Required Documents
- Current or most recent passport (original)
- NIC (original + copy)
- Birth certificate (original + copy)
- One recent passport-size photograph meeting ICAO specifications
- Police report (lost passport branch only)
- Payment receipt for the applicable fee category

## Steps
1. Complete the passport application form (Form K), available at the Department of
   Immigration and Emigration website or in person.
2. Book an appointment online through the Department's e-services portal (walk-ins
   are generally not accepted for renewals).
3. Attend the appointment with all required documents and biometrics capture at a
   Department of Immigration and Emigration office (Colombo Head Office or a
   regional office).
4. Pay the applicable fee based on the service tier chosen (normal, one-day, or
   urgent service).
5. Collect the new passport on the scheduled collection date, in person or via the
   selected delivery option.

## Fees
- Normal service (within ~12 working days): standard fee, verify current rate
- One-day service: higher fee, Colombo Head Office only
- Urgent service: highest fee tier

## Submission Location
- Department of Immigration and Emigration, Colombo Head Office, or a regional
  office (regional offices generally only offer normal service)
