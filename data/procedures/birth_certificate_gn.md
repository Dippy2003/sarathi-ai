# Birth Certificate via Grama Niladhari

## Eligibility / Branches
For obtaining a certified copy of a birth certificate for a birth already registered
in Sri Lanka. Two common cases:
- **Certified copy of an existing registration**: the birth was registered at the
  time it occurred; you just need a fresh certified copy.
- **Late registration**: the birth was never formally registered (common for births
  more than a certain number of years ago); this requires additional evidence and a
  GN investigation before a certificate can be issued.

## Required Documents
- Extract/reference number from the original registration, if known
- National ID of the applicant (or parent/guardian if applying on behalf of a minor)
- For late registration: evidence of the birth (e.g. hospital records, baptismal
  certificate, school admission records, affidavits from witnesses present at birth)

## Steps
1. Visit your area Grama Niladhari (GN) office and request a birth certificate copy
   application, specifying the registration district if known.
2. The GN verifies the request and, for a straightforward certified-copy request,
   forwards it to the Divisional Secretariat / District Registrar's office.
3. For late registration, the GN conducts a field inquiry and prepares a report
   supporting the registration before it is forwarded.
4. Submit the request with required documents at the Divisional Secretariat.
5. Pay the applicable fee.
6. Collect the certified birth certificate, either in person or by post.

## Fees
- Standard certified copy: nominal fee, verify current rate at the Divisional
  Secretariat
- Late registration: may involve additional processing fees

## Submission Location
- Area Grama Niladhari office, then the Divisional Secretariat / District
  Registrar General's office
