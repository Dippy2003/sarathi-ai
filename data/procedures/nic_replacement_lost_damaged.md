# NIC Replacement (Lost or Badly Damaged)

## Eligibility / Branches
For citizens whose NIC is **lost, stolen, or so badly damaged/illegible** that the
card itself cannot be used to prove identity or verify the printed details. If your
NIC is merely expiring or has minor legible damage, use NIC Renewal instead.

- **Lost/Stolen branch**: requires a police report before applying.
- **Badly damaged/illegible branch**: no police report required, but the damaged
  card (or remaining pieces) must be submitted with the application.

## Required Documents
- Police report confirming the loss (lost/stolen branch only)
- Damaged NIC or remaining fragments (damaged branch only)
- Birth certificate (original + copy)
- Two recent passport-size photographs
- Grama Niladhari certification of the application
- Affidavit or statutory declaration if requested by the officer

## Steps
1. If the card is lost or stolen, first report it to the nearest police station and
   obtain a written police report (this is mandatory before applying).
2. Obtain and complete the NIC replacement application form.
3. Get the form certified by your area's Grama Niladhari.
4. Submit the form with the police report (or the damaged card) and supporting
   documents at the District Secretariat or DRP Head Office, Battaramulla.
5. Pay the applicable replacement fee, which is higher than a normal renewal fee.
6. Collect a receipt with a reference number for tracking.
7. Collect the replacement NIC in person or by registered post.

## Fees
- Replacement fee is higher than the standard renewal fee; verify the current rate
  at submission, as lost-card replacement typically costs more than damaged-card
  replacement.

## Submission Location
- Nearest District Secretariat, or
- Department for Registration of Persons (DRP) Head Office, Battaramulla, for
  urgent service
