# Marriage Certificate via Grama Niladhari

## Eligibility / Branches
For obtaining a certified copy of a marriage certificate for a marriage already
registered in Sri Lanka. Applies to both:
- **Kandyan marriages** (registered under Kandyan Marriage and Divorce Act)
- **General marriages** (registered under the General Marriage Registration
  Ordinance), which follow a slightly different registration office chain.

If the marriage was never registered, that is a separate registration process, not
covered here.

## Required Documents
- NIC of the applicant
- Approximate date and place of marriage registration
- Names of both spouses as registered

## Steps
1. Visit your area Grama Niladhari (GN) office and request assistance identifying
   the correct Divisional Secretariat / Registrar of Marriages where the marriage
   was recorded.
2. Submit a request for a certified copy at that Divisional Secretariat, specifying
   the registration details.
3. Pay the applicable fee.
4. Collect the certified marriage certificate, either in person or by post.

## Fees
- Standard certified copy: nominal fee, verify current rate at the Divisional
  Secretariat

## Submission Location
- Divisional Secretariat / Registrar of Marriages for the district where the
  marriage was registered
